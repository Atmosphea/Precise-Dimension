# Privacy policy for Precise Dimension

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Precise Dimension does not collect, store, send or sell any personal information or browsing data.

- It runs only on the tab where you click its icon (or press its shortcut), and only until you turn it off.
- Everything it measures, draws and changes stays inside that tab, in memory. Refreshing the page clears it.
- Screenshots are made on your computer. They go to your clipboard or your downloads folder, nowhere else.
- The only thing it saves is your own settings (unit, fill strength, guides on or off), using the browser's built-in extension storage on your computer.
- It contains no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no code loaded from the internet.
- It makes no network requests of its own.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Atmosphea/Precise-Dimension/issues
